# Graphxify content roadmap

These recommendations are based on the 6 existing posts, the 4 service pages and the 6 case studies. **Quality over volume:** about 1–2 genuinely useful pieces per month, each grounded in Graphxify's own work, beats 50 generic articles. Don't auto-publish anything.

**Before writing anything new:** fix the flagged claims in the existing 6 posts (see [CONTENT-TRUST-AUDIT.md](CONTENT-TRUST-AUDIT.md)). Improving what's already indexed is the cheapest win.

Existing coverage, for context: choosing an agency; mobile-first; brand identity; custom vs WordPress; website ROI; local SEO.

**Not recommended until proof exists:** city/location pages, "best agency in X" pages, sports/fitness/trades industry guides, and AI-generated listicles.

**Each new article should:** name a real author (or keep "Graphxify Team" honestly), cite primary sources for any statistic, link to one relevant service and one relevant case study, use a real cover image with a descriptive alt, and be published via the CMS. Then redeploy and submit it to IndexNow (see [INDEXNOW-SETUP.md](INDEXNOW-SETUP.md)).
